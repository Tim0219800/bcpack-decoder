## microsoft/silicon-rans

`src/verilog_ref.rs` (test only) contains the probability tables `freq_table`
(alphabet sizes 1 to 8 bits) of `mdu_minirans.v` from
https://github.com/microsoft/silicon-rans (commit 2f6827d), used to check that
the tables generated by `src/model.rs` match them. The record header layout and
the flush bit described in `docs/SPEC.md` were also cross-checked against that
sample.
